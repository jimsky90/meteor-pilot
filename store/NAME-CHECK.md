## Trademarks
- **No "METEOR PILOT" trademark** came up in any search.
- Word "METEOR" alone: registered marks exist for unrelated goods, for example [US Reg. 5946770](https://trademarks.justia.com/792/49/meteor-79249480.html) for valves and industrial machine parts (Class 9), plus other "Meteor" filings ([example](https://markinton.com/trademark/meteor-85904835)). A common word used for unrelated goods doesn't block a two-word game name.
- **SPACEPILOT** (Class 9) was filed in April 2025 and **abandoned** in December 2025 ([record](https://markinton.com/trademark/spacepilot-99114315)). It's dead, and a different name anyway.

## Two quick checks for you (5 minutes)
1. **Store search on your phone:** search `Meteor Pilot` in the App Store and in Google Play. If nothing is called exactly "Meteor Pilot", you're clear. The real test is **App Store Connect itself**: when you create the app record (`LAUNCH-PLAN.md`, B4), it tells you right away if the name is taken. Do it soon, because that also reserves the name.
2. **US trademark search:** go to `tmsearch.uspto.gov`, search `meteor pilot`, and look for live marks in **Class 9** (game software) or **Class 41** (online games). I expect none. A trademark lawyer's clearance search costs a few hundred dollars and is optional for a free indie game at this stage.
