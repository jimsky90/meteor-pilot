## 1. Apple App Store

### App name (limit 30)
```
Meteor Pilot: Space Arcade
```
**26 / 30 characters**

Why the ": Space Arcade" part: Apple's search reads the name, subtitle and keyword field. Words in the name carry the most weight, and "space" and "arcade" are what people actually type when looking for this kind of game. The home-screen label still says just "Meteor Pilot", because that comes from the app itself, not from the store name.

If you prefer a clean name, use `Meteor Pilot` (12 characters) and move "space arcade" into the subtitle (see alternative 3 below, adjusted).

### Keywords (limit 100, comma-separated, no spaces)
```
galaxy,offline,spaceship,rocket,endless,runner,neon,3d,fighter,flight,reflex,casual,star,daily,scifi
```
**100 / 100 characters.** No word repeats inside the field, and none repeats a word from the name or subtitle (meteor, pilot, space, arcade, dodge, asteroids, beat, bosses), because Apple already combines those with the keywords. A script checked this.

How Apple uses these: it combines words across your name, subtitle and keywords, so `space` (name) + `spaceship` and `galaxy` (keywords) + `dodge` (subtitle) can all match searches like "space dodge game" or "galaxy runner". Plurals are matched automatically, so `star` also covers "stars".

Words left out on purpose:
- `shooter`: the most popular word in this genre, but your ship doesn't shoot. Apple can reject keywords that misdescribe the app, and players who expected shooting leave bad reviews.
- `alien`, `ufo`: there are no aliens in the game.
- Other games' names: not allowed.
- `game`, `games`, `free`: Apple already matches games by category, and these words are too crowded to rank for.

Swap-in candidates if you want to test changes after launch: `ship`, `orbit`, `hyper`, `retro`, `synthwave`, `combo`, `survival`, `bullet`.

### How the stores read your text
- **Apple** indexes the app name, subtitle and the hidden 100-character keyword field. It combines words across them, so don't repeat words, and separate keywords with commas without spaces. Plurals and category names are handled automatically, and other apps' trademarks aren't allowed. ([AppDrift: iOS keywords field guide 2026](https://appdrift.co/blog/ios-app-store-keywords-field), [Sonar: what the keyword field indexes](https://trysonar.app/blog/app-store-keyword-field), [AppLaunchFlow: keyword field guide 2026](https://www.applaunchflow.com/blog/app-store-keyword-field-guide-2026))
- **Google Play** has no keyword field. It reads the title, the short description and the full description. Use important phrases naturally a few times, and don't stuff keywords. ([AppDrift: Google Play description optimization](https://appdrift.co/blog/google-play-description-optimization), [AppLaunchFlow: Google Play optimization 2026](https://www.applaunchflow.com/blog/google-play-store-optimization-2026), [Sonar: Google Play long description structure](https://trysonar.app/blog/google-play-long-description-keyword-structure))
- **Game ASO in general:** screenshots and the first three lines of the description drive downloads more than keywords do. ([Sonar: game ASO](https://trysonar.app/blog/game-aso), [FoxData: hyper-casual ASO](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/how-to-grow-your-hypercasual-game-organically-with-aso/))

### Keywords worth targeting (in priority order)
1. **space game / space arcade.** Broad and high-volume. Covered by the name.
2. **asteroid / meteor dodge.** Matches the game exactly and isn't very crowded. Covered by the name and subtitle.
3. **offline game.** Very high volume, and true for this game. In the keywords and the Google description.
4. **spaceship / galaxy / rocket.** Popular words in the genre. In the keywords.
5. **endless runner.** People who like one-more-run games search for it. In the keywords.
6. **boss / daily challenge.** Smaller niches, and they describe the game's best features well.

Skipped: **space shooter** (huge, but not accurate for this game) and **bullet hell** (only the boss fights fit it).

### After launch
- Apple: try different screenshots with **Product Page Optimization** in App Store Connect. On Google, use **Store listing experiments**.
- Check which searches bring players in (App Store Connect > App Analytics > Sources; Play Console > Store performance), then swap the weakest keyword every 4 to 6 weeks.

## Things to fix first (before this copy is 100% true)
1. **The Supporter Pass isn't cosmetic.** In the game code, the $4.99 Supporter Pass gives "+25% XP on every run, forever", and the Shop says "Everything here is cosmetic, except the Supporter Pass XP boost." Your audit (`docs/EXPERT-AUDIT-v25.md`, bug 2) found that this lets paying players unlock ships and upgrades faster. This copy doesn't claim purchases are "cosmetic only". It says "no loot boxes" and "coins are earned by playing", which are true today. If you remove the XP boost (recommended), you can add "Paid items are cosmetic only" to the "Fair by design" section.
2. **"Plays offline" has a condition on Android.** With the Google Play (Trusted Web Activity) version, the first launch needs internet, because the game downloads itself then. The Google copy says "after the first launch". The Apple version, built with the game files inside the app, works offline from the start.
3. **The shop's test checkout shouldn't be in store builds.** Neither store allows prices for digital items unless you use its own payment system. A "Complete test purchase" button with $4.99 next to it would likely get the Apple version rejected, and could cause trouble on Google Play. See `LAUNCH-PLAN.md` part C. Hide the Store tab in store builds until real payments are on. The copy above doesn't mention paid items, so it stays true either way.
